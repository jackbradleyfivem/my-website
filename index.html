What we actually do

The old site listed this as a paragraph. Here it is as a way to choose.

01

Plated and buffet catering

Indoor rooms and outdoor functions, from a modest guest list to a large banquet. One hot dish comes with salad, vegetables, starch and gravy or sauce.

02

Weddings

Stretch tent, Tiffany chairs, table décor, flowers, crockery, glassware, a fruit cake and the service team. You buy the groceries for the three-course menu.

03

Drop-off

The cooking is done for you. Useful when you want the food handled and the gathering left in your own hands.

04

Breakfasts, cocktails, platters

Continental and hot breakfasts, finger food, and snack platters for offices and receptions. Platters are a snack, not a full lunch.

05

Kids’ parties

Stretch tent, jumping castle, décor, a party pack, a cake with a picture, and a children’s menu you build before we quote.

06

Crew and a mobile kitchen

Bartenders, setup, service and cleaning. Caravan kitchens for sports days and other outdoor functions, plus a working kitchen for ready meals, takeaway and sit-down.
